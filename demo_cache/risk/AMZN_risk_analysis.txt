**5. Value at Risk (VaR) Interpretation**

VaR estimates the potential loss over a specific time horizon at a given confidence level under normal market conditions.
*   **Daily VaR (95%) of -2.63%:** An investor holding AMZN stock can be 95% confident that their maximum loss on any given trading day will not exceed 2.63% of the investment's value. Conversely, there is a 5% chance (expected about 1 day in 20 trading days) that the daily loss could be *worse* than 2.63%.
*   **Daily VaR (99%) of -4.40%:** Increasing the confidence level to 99% gives a larger potential loss estimate. There is a 1% chance (expected about 1 day in 100 trading days) that the daily loss could exceed 4.40%.
*   **Conditional VaR (CVaR) (95%) of -4.22%:** This metric, also known as Expected Shortfall, provides insight into the severity of losses *when* they exceed the VaR threshold. If a day falls within the worst 5% of outcomes (i.e., the loss is greater than the 95% VaR of 2.63%), the *average* loss on those days is expected to be 4.22%. This highlights the magnitude of potential losses during significant negative market events for the stock.
